## Features
- [Stop hopping between reports](https://segmetrics.io/feature/multi-report-tables/): Multi\-Report Tables combine metrics from multiple reports into one table\. No more switching tabs, exporting to spreadsheets, or mentally connecting the dots yourself\. Add calculated columns for custom metrics and watch it all update automatically\.
- [Never chase your data again](https://segmetrics.io/feature/data-monitors/): Data Monitors keeps you in the loop\. Get pinged the second a metric needs attention and have your dashboards delivered to any inbox or Slack channel — automatically\. So relax\. You’ll know the moment any KPI needs your attention\.
- [Get everyone on the same page](https://segmetrics.io/feature/collaborative-dashboards/): Stop the “that's not what my dashboard says” debate before it starts\. Give your team unlimited access to the same live dashboards — and share secure public links with clients — so everyone's measuring the exact same thing, always\. 
- [Track AI Traffic All the Way to Revenue](https://segmetrics.io/feature/ai-search-tracking/): Most tools can only tell you that AI traffic showed up — not whether it's worth $10 or $10,000\. SegMetrics tracks AI traffic as its own channel automatically, connecting every ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity visit to real leads, revenue, and lifetime value\.
- [Truth serum for your ad platforms](https://segmetrics.io/feature/chrome-extension/): Google and Facebook love to stretch the truth\. SegMetrics’ Chrome Extension drops accurate ROAS, lead value, and lifetime attribution straight into your ad manager\. No more guessing\. No more cross\-referencing tabs\. Just the facts\.
